# Code of conduct

Charon should be a welcoming, practical place to collaborate. Everyone who
participates in the repository is expected to help keep discussions respectful,
focused, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and constructive, including when disagreeing.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's personal information or
  Charon content.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, moderation, and whether a
  contribution fits the project.
- Give credit where it is due and correct mistakes openly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing private information without
permission are not accepted. Deliberately exposing user content, credentials,
or security-sensitive details is also unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project spaces. Simon Hazard (`@SimonHazard`) is the principal maintainer and
is responsible for interpretation and enforcement. Responses may include a
warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion,
depending on severity and context.

To report conduct privately, use [GitHub private vulnerability
reporting](https://github.com/SimonHazard/Charon/security/advisories/new) and
begin the title with `Conduct report`. Reports will be handled as privately as
the circumstances allow. Do not include unrelated user content, credentials,
or sensitive paths.
